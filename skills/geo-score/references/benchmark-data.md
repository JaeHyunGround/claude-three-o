## Improvement Velocity

Typical score improvement timelines:
- Technical fixes (llms.txt, schema): +5-10 points in 2-4 weeks
- Content optimization (citability): +8-15 points in 4-8 weeks
- Entity building (Wikipedia, Wikidata): +10-20 points in 8-16 weeks
- Full GEO strategy: +20-35 points in 3-6 months

## Score Delta Significance

| Delta | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| ±0-2 | Within noise / no meaningful change |
| ±3-5 | Minor shift — monitor but not alarming |
| ±6-10 | Significant change — investigate cause |
| ±11-20 | Major shift — likely algorithm or content change |
| ±21+ | Dramatic change — verify data accuracy first |
