## Naver Ecosystem Components

### Naver Blog (블로그)
- Often ranks above external websites for informational queries
- Requires separate content strategy
- Naver Blog optimization ≠ website SEO

### Naver Cafe (카페)
- Community forums indexed by Naver
- User-generated content influences brand visibility
- Monitor brand mentions in popular cafes

### Naver Place (플레이스)
- Primary local business discovery in Korea
- Equivalent to Google Business Profile
- Reviews, photos, menu, booking all within Naver
- Critical for restaurant, clinic, academy, salon industries

### Naver Smart Store (스마트스토어)
- Naver's native e-commerce platform
- Product feed format differs from Google Merchant Center
- Products from Smart Store get preferential SERP placement
- Integration with Naver Pay for frictionless conversion

### Naver Search Advisor (서치어드바이저)
- Equivalent to Google Search Console
- Site verification, indexing request, crawl diagnostics
- Naver-specific sitemap submission
- Different from Google's indexing API

## Korean Content Analysis Notes

### Character vs Byte Length
- Korean characters: 3 bytes in UTF-8, 1 character visually
- Title tag: 30 Korean characters ≈ 60 English characters in display
- Meta description: 80 Korean characters optimal (vs 155 English)

### Korean Morphological Analysis
- Korean is agglutinative: 단어+조사+어미 combine into single tokens
- Keyword density calculation must account for morphological variants
- Example: "서울 맛집" can appear as "서울맛집", "서울의 맛집", "서울에서 맛집"

### Korean E-E-A-T Signals
- Professional certifications carry high weight (의사, 변호사, 세무사 etc.)
- Government registrations (사업자등록번호) are trust signals
- Korean review platforms (네이버 리뷰, 카카오맵 리뷰) matter more than Google Reviews
- Korean press coverage (네이버 뉴스) is a strong authority signal
