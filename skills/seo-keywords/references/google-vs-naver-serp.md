# Google vs Naver SERP Feature Comparison

## Feature Mapping

| Google Feature | Naver Equivalent | Notes |
|---------------|-----------------|-------|
| Featured Snippet | VIEW tab top result | Naver favors its own platforms |
| People Also Ask | Related searches (연관 검색어) | Shown at bottom of SERP |
| Knowledge Panel | Knowledge snippet (지식백과) | Pulls from Naver Encyclopedia |
| Local Pack (3-pack) | Place section (플레이스) | Naver Place is more prominent |
| Shopping results | Shopping section (쇼핑) | Smart Store gets priority |
| News carousel | News section (뉴스) | Only Naver News partners appear |
| Image pack | Image section (이미지) | Similar behavior |
| Video carousel | Video section | YouTube + Naver TV |
| AI Overview | CLOVA X summary | Limited rollout |

## Click Behavior Differences

| Metric | Google (Korea) | Naver |
|--------|---------------|-------|
| Organic CTR rank 1 | ~28% | ~15% (diluted by platform content) |
| Zero-click rate | ~45% | ~65% (more content consumed in-SERP) |
| Average session depth | 1.8 pages | 2.3 pages (within Naver ecosystem) |

## Strategy Implications

- Google: Focus on traditional SEO (backlinks, content, technical)
- Naver: Focus on platform presence (Blog, Place, Smart Store) alongside website
- Both: Schema markup benefits visibility on both engines
- Naver-first industries: restaurants, clinics, academies, local services
- Google-first industries: SaaS, global brands, technical/developer content
