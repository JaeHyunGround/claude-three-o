# Naver Ranking Factors

## Naver SERP Structure

Naver blends multiple content sources into a single SERP, unlike Google's link-based results.

| Tab/Section | Content Source | Priority |
|-------------|---------------|----------|
| VIEW (뷰) | Naver Blog + Cafe posts | High for informational queries |
| Website (웹사이트) | External websites | Medium, growing importance |
| News (뉴스) | Naver News partners | High for current events |
| Shopping (쇼핑) | Smart Store + partner malls | High for product queries |
| Place (플레이스) | Naver Place listings | High for local queries |
| Knowledge-iN (지식iN) | Q&A platform | Medium for how-to queries |
| Image (이미지) | All sources | Low-medium |

## Key Ranking Signals

### Blog/Cafe Content
- Recency (newer posts rank higher)
- Engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares)
- Blog authority (visitor count, post frequency)
- Content length and quality
- Image/video inclusion

### Website Content
- Naver Search Advisor verification
- Sitemap submission freshness
- Content relevance and depth
- Mobile optimization
- SSL/HTTPS

### Shopping/Product
- Smart Store integration level
- Product review count and rating
- Price competitiveness
- Sales volume
- Naver Pay support

## Naver-Specific Keyword Behavior

- Korean compound words split differently than Google
- Spacing variations matter: "서울맛집" vs "서울 맛집"
- Autocomplete suggestions differ from Google
- Seasonal trends follow Korean calendar (설날, 추석, 수능)
