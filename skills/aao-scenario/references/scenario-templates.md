# Scenario Templates by Industry

## Universal Scenarios (All Industries)

| # | User Intent | Agent Question Chain |
|---|------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Discovery | "What is [brand]?" → "What do they offer?" → "Where are they?" |
| 2 | Contact | "How do I reach [brand]?" → Phone/email/chat options |
| 3 | Hours | "Is [brand] open now?" → Hours check |
| 4 | Reviews | "Is [brand] good?" → Rating summary + key feedback |
| 5 | Location | "Where is [brand]?" → Address + directions |
| 6 | Pricing | "How much does [brand] cost?" → Price range or catalog |
| 7 | Comparison | "[brand] vs [competitor]?" → Feature comparison |
